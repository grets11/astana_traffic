How this forecast is built: the new avenues are trimmed to a shared observation window, because kabanbay_batyr joined the collector four days earlier than the others and its extra evening coverage would otherwise be read as a difference in traffic rather than in sampling. Three predictors compete on blocked time-series cross-validation — the tuned Ridge, a gradient-boosted tree, and a plain climatology grid — and the winner is chosen separately for the new and original avenues. A model only wins if its lead exceeds fold-to-fold noise; otherwise the climatology grid is used, because a lead smaller than its own standard deviation is not evidence. The chosen predictor is then evaluated at each of the next seven dates, so every row is a genuine forward prediction for that calendar day rather than a replay of history. The lag feature prev_congestion_score is deliberately excluded: there is no previous reading for a future hour, so any model relying on it cannot fill this grid. Free-flow speed is each avenue's own fastest observed reading, so 1.00× means "as fast as this road has ever been seen". Hours the collector has never sampled stay blank and unselectable. Cells outlined in amber rest on fewer than three underlying readings.
